Login con Device Key

¿Dónde está mi device_key? 
Windows: %AppData%\..\LocalLow\SavySoda\Pixel Starships\PlayerPrefs
  (busca "DeviceKey" o Registro: HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\SavySoda\Pixel Starships)

Linux/Steam Deck: ~/.config/unity3d/SavySoda/Pixel Starships/prefs
  (clave: persistedSteamDeviceKey, base64 del UUID)

El valor es un UUID como: xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx
